# Unit Keywords v0.1

This document defines the current prototype keyword meaning for units and skills.

## Combat Keywords

### `guard`

The unit is intended to specialize in defense, body-blocking, and reactive protection.

### `retaliate`

The unit may counterattack or punish melee attackers through skills or passive hooks.

### `push`

The unit may reposition enemies by force.

### `hack`

The unit manipulates resources, targeting logic, or enemy state.

### `shift`

The unit is allowed to reposition itself in a less standard way than simple movement.

### `debuff`

The unit applies reductions, jams, curses, or control effects.

### `curse`

The unit weakens enemy offense or stability through status-style pressure.

### `trap`

The unit can create delayed battlefield effects on cells.

### `ranged`

The unit is designed to operate beyond adjacent melee range.
